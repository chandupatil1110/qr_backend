Delete My Account

Request permanent removal of your account and personal data.

What gets deleted

When you submit a deletion request, we permanently remove all data associated with your mobile number from our servers, including:

  • Your profile (name, email, mobile number, age, address)
  • Your registered QR stickers and vehicle details
  • Emergency contacts you added
  • Scan history, alert events, and call logs on your QRs
  • Push notification tokens for your devices

Deletion is completed within 30 days of your request. You will receive an email confirmation once processed.

What we retain (and why)

A limited set of records is retained because Indian tax and financial regulations require it:

  • Payment records (Razorpay order/payment IDs, amount, timestamp) — retained for 8 years per Income Tax Act. These records do not include personal identifiers after account deletion.
  • Aggregated/anonymised usage statistics — no personal linkage, retained indefinitely.

All other data is deleted permanently and cannot be recovered.

Request deletion

Warning: Once deletion is processed, your active QR stickers will stop working — bystanders scanning them will see an "expired QR" page. This action is irreversible.
